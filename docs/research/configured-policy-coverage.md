# Configured policy coverage

Source-code audit: 2026-09-21. This covers all 148 policy records in the private export, retaining their input order.
The export is an inventory to investigate. Its descriptions, configured states, and edition notes are not enforcement evidence.
No configured values, account names, computer names, domain names, export timestamps, or private paths are included.

## Result

Direct routes: 14. Partial routes: 1. Related controls only: 16. Gaps: 117.

Direct means a matching registry address and staging route exist. It does not mean all options are exposed or enforcement is verified.
Partial means only part of a policy's value group is exposed. Related means similar user intent through another preference or scope.
Gap means no matching policy control was found. A gap is not a decision to implement or disable that policy.
Multiple records can map to one atomic setting. Counts measure exported records, not distinct app controls.

## Method and evidence

GitNexus concept queries identified module readers and factories. Literal searches then compared value names across production C# and JSON files.
Deletion markers were normalized to value names. Candidates were checked against registry constants, factories, and broker rules.
Registry names without a catalog route were not counted as coverage. Similar intent does not establish equivalent behavior.

## Verification still required

The scope column records the export's declared scope, not verified ADMX applicability.
Every row carries E. This audit did not verify installed behavior across Home, Pro, and Enterprise/Education.

- **E**: Verify current ADMX/CSP identity, applicable scope, minimum build, edition support, and organization-management precedence.
- **S**: Also verify supported security APIs, tamper protection, privileges, reboot behavior, and safe undo before exposing a control.
- **L**: Also verify whether the underlying feature and policy still exist on supported Windows builds.
- **A**: Also verify AI feature availability, hardware gates, and whether the policy targets the installed app or an older Windows integration.

Code groups Enterprise/Education into one tier. Research must distinguish unavailable, unverified, and organization-managed states.
Owner Mode cannot add unsupported policy behavior. A successful write does not prove that Windows honors it.
